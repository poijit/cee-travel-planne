🗺️ AI Travel Planner

An AI-powered web application that generates personalized, day-by-day travel itineraries based on destination, duration, budget, and interests. Users sign in via GitHub, generate trips using cutting-edge LLMs, visualize costs and weather on an interactive dashboard, and save their favorite itineraries to the cloud.
